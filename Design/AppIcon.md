## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon for Revenue, an iOS and macOS revenue analytics dashboard. Create a single square 1024x1024 icon artwork. Full bleed deep midnight navy background, subtle refined depth. Center a bold, beautifully proportioned ascending bar-chart emblem made of four thick vertical rounded columns, sharing a baseline, rising left to right. Colors correspond to revenue sources: warm orange, mint green, periwinkle blue, soft violet. Soft luminous material, restrained dimensional bevel and gentle ambient light, sophisticated native Apple app aesthetic. The emblem must remain unmistakable at 32px, generous balanced negative space, simple strong silhouette. No letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no arrows, no grid, no border, no extra objects, no watermark. Background fills every pixel; do not draw an outer rounded square or any exterior margin, as the OS applies icon masking. Deliver only the icon artwork.
